Time tracking

Track billable and non-billable time with a live timer or manual entries, review and edit everything before submission, and turn approved time into invoices in two clicks.

The live timer

  • Start a timer from a case (or from Time Reports) with a description of what you're doing.
  • The floating timer stays visible on every page while it runs — it is stored server-side, so it keeps counting even if you close the tab or switch devices.
  • You can pause / resume, edit the description or case while running, and stop to save the accumulated time as a draft entry, or discard it.

Manual entries

  • Add an entry with a case, description, duration (minutes), and date — for work you did away from the screen.
  • Each saved entry gets a timestamp, and the list shows the most recent entries at the top.
  • Entries can be marked billable or non-billable and can carry a custom hourly rate.

Editing before submission

Draft and submitted entries can be edited — change the case, description, duration, or date — or deleted, right up until they are invoiced. Once an entry is attached to an invoice it is locked to keep billing consistent.

Entry workflow

StatusMeaning
DraftJust recorded; fully editable.

Hourly rates

Every lawyer sets their hourly rate (in the firm's currency) in Time Reports. The rate is stored with each entry when it's created, so historical entries keep the rate that applied at the time. Individual entries can override the rate.

Creating an invoice from time entries

  1. In Time Reports, select the submitted entries you want to bill (optionally filtered by case or client).
  2. Choose the client, a due date, and the VAT rate.
  3. Cliavo creates an invoice with one line per entry (description, hours × rate) in your firm's default currency, and marks the entries as invoiced.

See Billing & invoices for what happens next.

Reports & summaries

Time Reports shows totals by period, case, and biller — tracked vs. billable hours and their value — so you can review productivity before billing runs and catch unbilled work.
