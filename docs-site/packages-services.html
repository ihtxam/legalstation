Client packages & on-demand services

Subscription packages

Firm admins define packages (quotas, consultation hours, features, tier labels) under Packages. Clients can subscribe via the public subscribe page for your firm.

On-demand services

  • Publish a service catalog under Services.
  • Clients add items to a cart and check out.
  • The firm accepts the order; Cliavo can create a case and assign a lawyer.
