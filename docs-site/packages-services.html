Packages & services (Upselling)

Two recurring-revenue tools live under Upselling: subscription packages (legal protection plans billed monthly/yearly) and an on-demand services catalog (fixed-price legal products with a full order workflow).

Subscription packages

  • Each package has a name, description, feature list, and a highlight label (e.g. "Most popular").
  • Pricing per interval: monthly, 6-month, and yearly prices, plus a minimum commitment (e.g. 12 months).
  • Entitlements: cases per period, consultation hours per period, and included fixed-work hours.
  • Restrict which case types a package covers (e.g. only employment and tenancy law).
  • Toggle active (sellable) and public (visible on your website) separately, and order them with drag handles.

Subscribers

  • Clients subscribe from your public website (the package block links to registration) — the client record, portal account, and subscription are created together.
  • Subscriptions track status (active, past due, cancelled, expired), the current billing period, and the commitment end date.
  • Subscribed clients can change plan from their portal; entitlement usage (cases, hours) is visible per period.
  • When a subscriber opens a case, they complete an intake form so the lawyer starts with context.

On-demand services catalog

  • Each service has a name, description, category (advice, contract, documents, employment, corporate, other), and a fixed price.
  • Fulfilment type: document (you deliver files) or consultation (you deliver a meeting).
  • Set estimated effort hours, delivery notes, and the default case type used if the order becomes a case.
  • Like packages: active/public switches and manual ordering; public services appear automatically in the website Services block.

The order lifecycle

Clients buy services from their portal (or from your website after registering):

  1. Cart & checkout — the client picks services, adds a brief per item, and checks out. With Stripe/Adyen connected they pay online; otherwise the firm can mark the order paid manually.
  2. Intake — after payment the client submits the intake description so your team knows exactly what's needed.
  3. Acceptance — the firm reviews and accepts (optionally assigning a lawyer, and creating a linked case) or rejects with a reason.
  4. Delivery — the lawyer uploads deliverables (documents) or holds the consultation, with remarks for the client.
  5. Revisions — the client can request changes within the included revision rounds (default 2); each round is tracked.
  6. Completion — the order is completed and locked; every step is recorded in the order's event timeline.

Orders have human-readable numbers (e.g. ORD-2026-00012) and appear in the firm's order list with status filters, so nothing gets lost.

Currencies

Packages and services are priced in your firm's currency; orders carry their currency end-to-end. See multi-currency billing.
