Getting started

Cliavo is a complete practice-management platform for law firms. Every firm gets its own isolated workspace with its own branding, team, clients, cases, billing, and public website. This guide takes you from creating an account to a fully working firm.

Create your workspace

There are two ways a firm workspace is created:

  • Self-serve trial — click Start free trial on cliavo.com, enter your firm name, your name, email, and a password. Your workspace is created instantly with a 15-day trial on the starter plan, and you are taken straight to onboarding.
  • Provisioned by Cliavo — our team creates the firm for you and sends the owner a credentials email with a temporary password. On first login you are asked to set your own password before continuing.

The onboarding wizard

After signup you complete a short wizard. Everything can be changed later in Settings.

  • 1 · Firm profile — legal name, address, contact email, phone, and VAT/UID number. These appear on your invoices.
  • 2 · Branding — upload your logo and pick your primary and secondary brand colors. They style your workspace, client portal, and public website.
  • 3 · Currency & tax — the default billing currency for new invoices and packages (CHF, EUR, USD, GBP, AED, SAR, and more) and your default VAT rate (Swiss standard is 8.1%).
  • 4 · Subdomain — pick your firm address, e.g. yourfirm.cliavo.com. Lawyers and clients sign in there, and your public site is also reachable at cliavo.com/site/yourfirm immediately, no DNS needed. A custom domain (e.g. portal.yourfirm.ch) can be connected later in Settings → Workspace.

Signing in

  • Firm members and clients sign in with email + password at /login (or your firm subdomain).
  • If two-factor authentication is enabled, you enter a 6-digit authenticator code after your password.
  • If your account was created with a temporary password, you are asked to set a new one before you can continue. You do not need to re-type the temporary password.
  • Platform operators (Cliavo staff) use a separate login at /platform/login; superadmin accounts can never enter a firm workspace through the normal login.

Recommended first steps

  • Invite your team from Settings → Team: sub-admins, lawyers, and assistants each get an email invite. See Team & roles.
  • Add banking details (IBAN or QR-IBAN plus creditor address) in Settings so Swiss QR-bills can be generated on invoices.
  • Set your hourly rate in Time Reports so tracked time is valued correctly.
  • Create your first client and case — see Clients & CRM and Cases & matters.
  • Publish your website — pick a template in CMS and go live in minutes. See Website (CMS).

Languages

The interface is available in English, French, German, Italian, and Arabic (with full right-to-left layout for Arabic). Each user picks their own language from the language switcher; invitation emails can also be sent in the recipient's language. Platform administrators control which languages are enabled.

Trial, plans & account

  • Your trial banner shows the days remaining. When the trial ends, the workspace is locked (read-only access to account and support) until a plan is activated.
  • Under Account you can see your current plan, price, and billing cycle, and upgrade at any time.
  • If your firm is suspended or payment is past due, admins keep access to Account, Support, and plan checkout so they can restore service.

Mobile app

Cliavo has a companion mobile app (iOS and Android) for lawyers and clients: dashboards, cases, invoices, and document scanning with the camera. It signs in against https://cliavo.com with the same email and password.
