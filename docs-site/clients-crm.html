Clients & CRM

The Clients area is your firm's address book and lightweight CRM: every person or company you work for, their contact details, their cases, invoices, activities, and portal access.

Individual and company clients

  • Individuals — first/last name, date of birth, and contact details. A first or last name is required.
  • Companies — company name (required), registration number, and a contact person.
  • Both types share email, phone, full address (street, city, postal code, country), and free-form notes.

Clients have a status: invited (created but no portal account yet), active, or inactive. Admins, sub-admins, and lawyers can create clients; the client list is searchable and each client has a detail page with an inline Edit form.

Creating clients where you need them

Besides the Clients page, you can create a client on the fly from the Assign client dialog inside a case — switch the toggle from Existing client to Create client, fill the short form, and the new client is created and assigned to the case in one step.

Activities, notes & reminders

Each client has an activity timeline for day-to-day relationship work:

  • Notes — meeting summaries or anything worth remembering.
  • Meetings — with a date, so they appear in your agenda.
  • To-dos and next actions — assignable to a team member with a due date.
  • Reminders — pick a remind-at time to be nudged about follow-ups.
  • You can @mention teammates and mark items complete when done.

Leads pipeline

The CRM section includes a leads pipeline for potential clients who are not (yet) clients. Each lead has a contact name, email, phone, company, source, notes, and an assigned team member, and moves through stages:

StageMeaning
NewJust came in (e.g. from your website contact form).
ContactedYou reached out.
QualifiedReal potential — worth pursuing.
ConsultationA first consultation is booked or done.
ProposalYou sent an engagement proposal or quote.
Won / LostOutcome of the pursuit.

When a lead is won, use Convert to turn it into a real client (and optionally a first case) without retyping anything. Leads submitted through your public website's contact form land in this pipeline automatically — see Website & leads.

Giving clients portal access

Clients can receive their own login to the client portal. From Settings → Team (or the client page), send a client invite linked to the client record; they set their own password and see only their own cases, invoices, and documents. See Invitations.

Tip: subscription clients (see Packages) can register themselves from your public website — the client record and portal account are created automatically.
