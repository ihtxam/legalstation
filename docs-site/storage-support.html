Storage, announcements & support

Storage quota

Each firm has a document storage limit (default 10 GB; common presets 2 / 10 / 50 GB). Usage appears in Settings → Firm. Uploads are blocked when the quota is exceeded — contact Cliavo to upgrade.

Platform announcements

Cliavo may show popup announcements in your workspace (new features, maintenance, unpaid bills). Dismiss them after reading; critical messages may reappear until resolved.

Support tickets

Firm admins can open tickets from Support (bug reports, issues, screenshots, sensitivity). Each ticket gets a number (e.g. TKT-2026-00001). Statuses include open, processing, under review, responded, resolved, and closed. Reply in the same thread; the bell icon shows updates. Resolved tickets auto-close after 7 days without a firm reply. Monthly ticket limits are set by Cliavo.

Contact support

  • In-app: open Support in the sidebar.
  • Email: support@cliavo.com
  • Docs: this Help Center at docs.cliavo.com (also available at /help on your Cliavo host).
