Support & storage

How to reach Cliavo when something's unclear, how support tickets work, what platform announcements are, and how document storage limits behave.

Support tickets

  • Firm admins open tickets under Support with a subject, description, and a sensitivity level (low → critical).
  • Attach up to 5 files per ticket (documents or screenshots, max 1 MB each) to show exactly what you see.
  • Tickets get a number like TKT-2026-00042 and a status: open → processing → under review → responded → resolved → closed.
  • Replies from Cliavo appear in the ticket thread and are flagged unread in your sidebar until you view them; you can reply back with more details or attachments.
  • Resolved tickets close automatically after a few days if you don't reply; there is a fair-use monthly ticket limit per firm.

Platform announcements

  • Cliavo occasionally posts announcements (maintenance, new features) that appear as a banner/popup in your workspace.
  • Severities: info, warning, critical. Audience is either firm admins only or all members.
  • Click Got it to dismiss — an announcement won't reappear once dismissed, and announcements published before your account existed are never shown to you.

Storage quotas

  • Case document storage counts against your firm's storage quota (set by your plan).
  • Per-file size and file-type rules are configurable — see Settings → Upload policy.
  • When the quota is reached, uploads fail with a clear message; delete old files or ask support for a higher quota.
  • Support-ticket attachments do not count against firm storage.

Other ways to reach us

  • Email: support@cliavo.com
  • This help center: cliavo.com/help — use the search bar at the top of every page.
