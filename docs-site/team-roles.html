Team & roles

Bring your whole firm into one workspace with role-based access: admins run the firm, sub-admins help manage it, lawyers work their cases, and assistants support the team.

The four firm roles

RoleTypical personDefault access
AdminOwner / managing partnerEverything: all cases, billing, settings, team, roles, website, upselling.
Sub-adminOffice managerFirm-wide cases and clients, invites, most management screens; not firm-critical settings.
LawyerAssociate / partnerTheir assigned cases, own time tracking, invoicing, clients.
AssistantParalegal / secretarySupport access to assigned cases; no billing or settings by default.

Clients are not firm members — they get portal accounts instead.

Customising role permissions

Under Settings → Roles, admins can fine-tune what each role can do per area — for example whether lawyers see all firm cases or only their own, who can create invoices, manage the website, or invite people. Overrides apply to your firm only and can be reset to platform defaults with one click.

Invitations

  • Invite teammates by email with a chosen role; client invites are linked to a client record.
  • Pick the email language per invite (EN/FR/DE/IT/AR).
  • The invitee opens the link, sets their name and password, and lands in the workspace — invitations expire after a while and can be re-sent.
  • If the invitee already has a Cliavo account (same email), they can accept the invite while signed in and join the firm with their existing login.

Managing members

  • The Team page lists every member with their role and title.
  • Each lawyer manages their own hourly rate; admins can see rates for billing purposes.
  • Lawyers are assignable to cases, tasks, leads, and service orders.
Tip: give receptionists the assistant role and grant only what they need in Settings → Roles — the permission matrix beats sharing an admin login every time.
