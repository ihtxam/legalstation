Documents & messages

Keep every pleading, contract, and piece of evidence on the case it belongs to, request documents from clients with a deadline, and communicate through secure case messaging instead of email threads.

Uploading documents

  • Upload files on the case page (or let clients upload through the portal / mobile app camera scan).
  • Each document stores its file name, type, size, an optional description, and who uploaded it.
  • Documents can be organised into folders per case.
  • Downloads are served through authenticated links — no public URLs.

Upload rules & quotas

  • Firms have a per-file upload size limit and an allowed file-type list (e.g. PDF, Word, images) — see Settings → Upload policy.
  • Each firm also has a total storage quota; uploads beyond the quota are refused with a clear message. Quotas are set per plan and can be adjusted by Cliavo.
  • Support-ticket attachments have their own compact limit (1 MB per file) and don't count against firm storage.

Document requests

Stop chasing clients by email — request exactly what you need:

  • Create a request on a case with a title, description, and due date (e.g. "Signed power of attorney").
  • The client sees the request in their portal and uploads the file directly against it.
  • The request is marked fulfilled with a link to the uploaded document, or can be cancelled if no longer needed.

Case messaging

  • Every case has a private message thread between the firm and the client(s) on that case.
  • Clients write from their portal; lawyers reply from the case page. Replies support threading.
  • Unread counters in the sidebar show where something is waiting for you, and messages can be marked read.
  • Messages stay attached to the matter forever — a clean, auditable record of client communication.

Document intelligence

Where AI features are enabled, uploaded documents can be analysed to extract summaries and key facts to speed up review. Ask Cliavo support if you want this switched on for your firm.
