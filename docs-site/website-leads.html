Website (CMS) & leads

Every firm gets a public website built with the CMS page builder — no web agency needed. Pages are composed from ready-made blocks, edited with a live preview, styled with your branding, and published at your subdomain or custom domain.

The page editor

  • Create a page from CMS: it opens a full-screen editor with the block list on one side and a live preview on the other.
  • Reorder blocks by drag and drop (grip handle) — the preview updates instantly.
  • Every page has a title, a URL slug, a published switch, and a homepage switch (one page is your site's front page).
  • The SEO tab sets the meta title and description used by search engines.

Block types

BlockWhat it shows
HeroBig headline, subtitle, and a call-to-action button.
TextHeaded rich-text section for any content.
FeaturesGrid of feature cards (icon, title, text).
StatsKey numbers — years of experience, cases won, clients served.
TestimonialsClient quotes with names.
TeamYour lawyers with photos, roles, and bios.
FAQQuestion/answer accordion.
GalleryImage grid.
Logo stripBar of association / client logos.
PackagesLive block — automatically renders your published subscription packages.
ServicesLive block — automatically renders your published on-demand services.
ContactContact form that creates a lead in your CRM.

List-style blocks (features, stats, testimonials, team, FAQ, gallery, logos) support adding and removing items freely. The Packages and Services blocks pull live data from Upselling — publish a new package and your website updates by itself.

Templates

Start from a template instead of a blank page: Classic, Minimal, Modern, Services & packages, Team, or Contact. Templates pre-fill a sensible block layout you then edit.

Branding

The public site uses your logo and brand colors from Settings → Branding, so the website, workspace, and client portal all match.

Where your site is published

  • Immediately (no DNS needed): https://cliavo.com/site/your-slug
  • Your subdomain: https://your-slug.cliavo.com — also where your team and clients sign in.
  • Custom domain: connect e.g. portal.yourfirm.ch in Settings → Workspace with a single CNAME record. See Domains.

Lead capture

  • The Contact block on your public pages feeds submissions straight into your CRM leads pipeline with source "website".
  • Package and service blocks link visitors to subscription registration and service checkout, turning your website into a storefront.
Tip: keep one published homepage plus a few focused pages (About, Services, Contact). Search engines read your SEO title/description per page — write them like an ad for the page.
