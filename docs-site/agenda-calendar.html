Agenda & calendar

One agenda for everything with a date on it: case deadlines, tasks that are due, client meetings and reminders, your own personal events — and, if you connect an external calendar, your Google / Outlook / iCloud events too.

What appears in your agenda

  • Case deadlines — from the deadline field on cases.
  • Task due dates — case tasks assigned to you.
  • Client activities — meetings, to-dos, and reminders with dates.
  • Personal events — created directly in the calendar with title, location, start/end, or all-day.
  • Imported events — pulled from connected external calendars.

Connecting external calendars

Each user can connect their own calendars under Calendar → Connections:

ProviderHow it connects
Google CalendarOAuth — sign in with Google and grant calendar access.
Microsoft / OutlookOAuth — Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com account.
Apple iCloudCalDAV with your Apple ID and an app-specific password (create one at appleid.apple.com).

Two-way sync

  • Choose the sync direction per connection: both ways, push only (Cliavo → external), or pull only (external → Cliavo).
  • Cliavo pushes deadlines, meetings, and personal events to the external calendar, and imports external events so your agenda shows real availability.
  • Sync runs on a schedule and can be triggered manually with Sync now. The connection shows the last sync time and any error.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted; disconnecting removes the link immediately.
Tip: lawyers who live in Outlook can set "push only" so court deadlines appear in Outlook, without their private appointments being imported into the workspace.
