Agenda & calendar sync

The Agenda shows deadlines, tasks, client activities, and personal events.

Connect an external calendar

  1. Open Settings → Calendar.
  2. Connect Google or Outlook (OAuth). Platform credentials are configured once by Cliavo superadmin for all firms.
  3. Or connect iCloud with an Apple ID + app-specific password.
  4. Choose two-way, push-only, or pull-only sync, then sync.

What syncs

  • Out: case deadlines, tasks, meetings, personal agenda events.
  • In: events from the connected calendar appear on Agenda (duplicates avoided when Cliavo already pushed them).
