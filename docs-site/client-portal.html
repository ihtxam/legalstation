Client portal

The client portal is your clients' own secure view of the firm: their cases, invoices, documents, messages, subscription, and service orders — branded with your logo and colors, available in five languages.

How clients get access

  • Invite from the firm — send a client invite (Settings → Team or the client page). The invite email links to a registration page where the client sets a name and password.
  • Self-registration via packages — visitors who subscribe to a package on your website get a portal account automatically.
  • Clients sign in at your firm login URL with email + password, and can enable 2FA like any user.

What clients see

  • Dashboard — active cases, open invoices, and recent activity at a glance.
  • Cases — only the cases they are attached to, with shared notes, status, and the case timeline.
  • Documents — files on their cases; they can upload documents themselves (e.g. responding to a document request).
  • Messages — the secure message thread per case, instead of email back-and-forth.
  • Invoices — issued invoices with amounts, due dates, status, and PDF download (with the Swiss QR part where applicable).
  • Subscription — their package, entitlements used this period, and plan switching.
  • Services — the firm's service catalog, cart, checkout, order tracking, intake forms, deliverable downloads, and revision requests.

What clients can never see

  • Other clients' cases, invoices, or documents — access is strictly scoped to their own records.
  • Internal case notes (only notes marked shared are visible).
  • Your team's time entries, rates, or internal tasks.

On mobile

The Cliavo mobile app has a client mode with the same scope: cases, invoices, documents (including camera scanning), and messages.

Tip: the portal lives at your firm URL — your-slug.cliavo.com or your custom domain — so clients always see your brand, not ours.
