Settings

Everything about your firm's configuration lives in Settings. Most tabs are admin-only; every user has their personal security and language preferences.

Firm profile

Legal name, address, contact email, phone, website, and VAT/UID number — printed on your invoices and shown on your public site's contact section.

Branding

  • Upload your logo (PNG/JPEG/WebP, up to 5 MB).
  • Pick primary and secondary colors — they style the workspace, client portal, emails, and the public website.

Currency & VAT defaults

  • The firm's default currency is used for new invoices, packages, services, and rate displays. Available currencies (CHF, EUR, USD, GBP, AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, BHD, OMR, JOD) are enabled platform-wide.
  • The default VAT rate pre-fills new invoices (Swiss standard 8.1%).
  • Swiss QR-bill banking: IBAN / QR-IBAN and creditor address. See QR-bills.

Workspace domains

  • Subdomain — your workspace and public site at your-slug.cliavo.com. Changing the slug changes your links, so pick carefully.
  • Custom domain — enter your domain (e.g. portal.yourfirm.ch) and create one CNAME record at your DNS provider pointing it at your Cliavo subdomain:
    TypeName (your domain)Value (points to)
    CNAMEportal.yourfirm.chyour-slug.cliavo.com
    Root domains without a subdomain (e.g. yourfirm.ch) use an A record to the Cliavo IP instead. DNS changes can take up to 24–48 hours.

Payment gateways

  • Adyen — connect your own merchant account (API key, merchant account, HMAC key, test/live environment). Webhooks update order payment status automatically.
  • Stripe — checkout for service orders where configured.
  • No gateway? Orders can be marked paid manually — nothing blocks your workflow.

Upload policy & storage

  • Per-file size limit and allowed file types for case documents.
  • A firm-wide storage quota; usage counts documents across all cases. Limits are tied to your plan and can be raised — contact support.

Team & roles

Invite members, set roles, and customise the role permission matrix. Covered in detail in Team & roles.

Personal preferences

  • Language — each user chooses EN/FR/DE/IT/AR; Arabic flips the layout to RTL.
  • Security — change password, enable/disable 2FA.
  • Calendar connections — see Agenda & calendar.

Account & plan

The Account page shows your subscription plan, price, billing cycle, trial days remaining, and lets you upgrade. If the workspace is locked (trial ended, suspended, past due), admins can still reach Account and Support to restore service.
