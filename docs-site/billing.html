Billing & invoices

Create invoices manually or from tracked time, bill in your client's currency, attach Swiss QR payment parts, split large amounts into installment plans, and follow every invoice from draft to paid.

Creating an invoice

  • Pick the client (and optionally the case), a due date, and the VAT rate (your firm default is pre-filled).
  • Add line items — each with a description, hourly or flat-fee billing type, quantity, and unit price. Totals and VAT are calculated automatically.
  • Invoices from time entries arrive with one line per entry, ready to adjust.
  • Invoices get a sequential number and can be edited while in draft.

Multi-currency billing

Your firm has a default currency (chosen during onboarding or in Settings), and each invoice carries its own currency. The platform supports CHF, EUR, USD, GBP, AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, BHD, OMR, and JOD (availability is controlled platform-wide). Amounts are always formatted with the right symbol and decimal rules for the currency.

Swiss QR-bills

  • Add your IBAN or QR-IBAN and creditor address (street, number, postal code, city, country) in Settings.
  • Invoice PDFs then include a compliant Swiss QR payment part that clients can scan in their e-banking.
  • QR-bills are generated for CHF and EUR invoices only (the Swiss standard does not allow other currencies); other currencies simply omit the QR part.
  • Installment invoices include a QR part per installment amount.

Payment plans (installments)

Split a large invoice into a schedule the client can afford:

  • Choose the number of installments (up to 12), the interval, and the amount per installment.
  • Cliavo can auto-generate an invoice per installment when it falls due, and optionally email it to the client automatically (a daily scheduled job takes care of this).
  • Mark installments paid as money arrives; the plan shows a timeline of what's paid, due, and upcoming.

Invoice PDFs & sending

Every invoice has a print-ready PDF (firm logo, address, line items, VAT breakdown, and the QR part where applicable). Invoices can be emailed to clients, and clients also see them in their portal.

Online payments

Connect Stripe or Adyen (Settings → Payments) to let clients pay service orders and invoices online. Firms can bring their own Adyen merchant account — including HMAC-verified webhooks — or use platform-level credentials. Without a gateway, orders can always be marked paid manually. See Settings → Payments.

Revenue overview

The dashboard shows outstanding, overdue, and collected amounts in your firm currency, and admin analytics break revenue down over time so you can spot trends.
