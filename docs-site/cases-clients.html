Cases & clients

Clients

Create individual or company clients, assign lawyers, and track related cases and invoices from the client record.

Cases (matters)

  • Open a case, set status, and attach the client.
  • Use stages and tasks to track progress.
  • Upload documents (subject to firm file-type / size policy and storage quota).
  • Message internally or with the client when portal access is enabled.

Client portal

Invited clients can view their cases, share documents, message the firm, see invoices, and use the agenda for relevant appointments.

Document requests

Staff can request specific documents from clients; clients upload into the linked case.
