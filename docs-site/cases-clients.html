Cases & matters

Cases (matters) are the heart of Cliavo: each case bundles its clients, assigned lawyers, documents, notes, tasks, time entries, messages, and deadlines in one place.

Creating a case

  • Title and an optional internal reference number.
  • Type — civil, criminal, corporate, family, real estate, employment, tax, immigration, intellectual property, or other.
  • Status — open, pending, closed, or archived.
  • Optional description, court name, court file number, and a deadline (deadlines appear in your agenda).
  • Attach one or more clients and assign lawyers right away, or later from the case page.

Clients and lawyers on a case

A case can have several clients and several lawyers. From the case detail page use Assign client (choose an existing client or create a new one inline) and Assign lawyer. Lawyers see the cases assigned to them; admins and sub-admins see all firm cases. Every assignment is recorded in the case timeline.

Matter stages (workflow board)

Firms can define their own matter stages — e.g. Intake → Research → Drafting → Court → Closed — each with a name, color, and order. Cases move through stages so you can see at a glance where every matter stands. Stages marked as closed stages indicate the matter is finished. Manage stages from the case board; drag to reorder.

Tasks & subtasks

  • Create tasks on a case with a title, description, assignee, and due date.
  • Tasks support subtasks (parent/child), @mentions, and manual ordering.
  • Statuses: to do → in progress → done (or cancelled). Completed tasks keep their completion timestamp.
  • Tasks can be linked to a matter stage so stage boards show outstanding work.

Notes & case timeline

Case notes have a title, rich text body, and a visibility setting: internal (team only) or shared (visible to the client in the portal). The case timeline (events) records everything that happens: creation, assignments, uploads, notes, status changes — a full audit trail per matter.

Client intake forms

For cases that come from subscription packages or on-demand services, clients complete a structured intake form: what happened, when, how, their involvement, the desired outcome, and a privacy level (standard, sensitive, private). The submission is attached to the case so the assigned lawyer starts with full context.

Cases from orders and disputes

On-demand service orders can be converted into cases automatically when accepted, carrying over the client, the service type's default case type, and the client's brief. See the order lifecycle.

Tip: use the global timer while working on a case — it follows you across pages and books tracked time straight onto the case. See Time tracking.
